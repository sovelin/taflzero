Self-play trends

      
      

    
Every self-play ending, the game length and the encirclement sizes, sorted by how strongly they move. Trend = weighted least-squares slope over the window (binomial weights for shares; for the length, weights = games and the noise taken from the scatter), its interval widened when the points scatter more than chance allows. With ~20 metrics one of them reaches |z| > 2 by chance about every other look, so only |z| ≥ 3 is called rising / falling, 2–3 is "maybe". Previous = the window of the same length just before. The sparkline is the last 40M positions.
